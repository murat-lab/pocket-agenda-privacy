Pocket Agenda — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

Pocket Agenda does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you write stays on your device.

Data stored

Notes, titles, color labels, reminder times and settings are stored locally in your Chrome profile using chrome.storage.local. This data is never sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests and has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

Permissions

  • storage – save your notes and settings on your device.
  • alarms – trigger the reminder times you choose.
  • notifications – show reminder notifications.
  • contextMenus – the "Add to Pocket Agenda" right-click action.

Exports

PDF, .ics and JSON backup files are created on your device and saved where you choose. The "Google Calendar" button opens a Google Calendar page in a new tab with the event details in the link; this only happens when you click it, and is then subject to Google's privacy policy.

Your control

You can delete notes at any time in the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data. Notes are not encrypted; please do not store passwords in them.

Children

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Pocket Agenda — Gizlilik Politikası

Pocket Agenda hiçbir kişisel veriyi toplamaz, göndermez, satmaz veya paylaşmaz. Yazdığınız her şey cihazınızda kalır. Notlar, başlıklar, renkler, hatırlatıcı saatleri ve ayarlar yalnızca Chrome profilinizde yerel olarak saklanır; geliştiriciye veya üçüncü taraflara iletilmez. Eklenti ağ isteği yapmaz; hesap, analiz veya reklam yoktur. "Google Takvim" düğmesine tıkladığınızda etkinlik bilgileri bağlantıyla Google Takvim sayfasında açılır; bu yalnızca sizin tıklamanızla olur. Notlar şifrelenmez, lütfen parola yazmayın.
